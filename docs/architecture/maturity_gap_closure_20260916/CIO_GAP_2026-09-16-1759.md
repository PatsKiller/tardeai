## 5. The gap in one paragraph

The four-channel split is **real and mostly served** — the corpus proves the DM is clean after
09-14 and two channels were never noisy. What remains is a **deployment lag and a verification
lag**: the B-phase code that closes D2–D4 is merged but not promoted, the one channel that was
demonstrably wrong has not been re-exported to prove the fix, governance is a signed document
rather than a failing test, the standing book never reaches the phone, and the single accountable
owner is a registered goal with a `wake_count` of zero. The path is fully ordered in `07`; phases
B–D are unstarted or mid-flight, and the first move is a promote, not new code.

# ADR-005 — One decision-integrity validator before any actionable surface; alert and wake semantics

Status: PROPOSED (branch `wt/schd-decision-integrity-20260925`, 2026-09-25) · extends v3.3 §9.3 / §20 / §24; supersedes nothing.
Authority: READ_ONLY_ADVISORY. MBI_BEHAVIOR = 0. No new financial authority is created by this record.

## Context
On 2026-09-25 10:48 ET the desk answered an SCHD re-entry question with "Plan: buy-limit in zone · stop $33.55 · target $35.30" at a price of $33.12 (below the stop), "fresh ✓ (0h)" under a 96-hour TTL, "wash blocked until Oct 8" for a taxable sale that was a gain, a held note for one of two accounts, and an offer to "watch it" that armed nothing. Each surface had its own partial rules (`cio_entry_state` blocks price≤stop but is only called by the entry-state runner; the desk's `invalidation` criterion tests only that a stop exists; the card built the plan line from the existence of stop/target). Quote-age limits disagreed across five modules (15 min, 4 h, 6 h, 72 h, 96 h). See `docs/_findings/SCHD_DECISION_INTEGRITY_INCIDENT_2026-09-25.md`.

## Decision
1. **One validator, reused not duplicated.** `scripts/lib/decision_integrity.py` (`DecisionIntegrity@v1`) composes `cio_entry_state.evaluate` (hard blocks incl. price ≤ stop), `watch_canonical_quote.derive_freshness` (session-aware quote age) and the holdings store, and adds the missing joins: plan version/age, tax-evidence class, alert arming, order semantics. States, in severity order: `MISSING_EVIDENCE`, `INVALIDATED_BY_PRICE_OR_STOP`, `STALE_OR_CONFLICTING_QUOTE`, `STALE_PLAN`, `HELD_POLICY_BLOCK`, `HOUSE_WASH_HOLD`, `TAX_STATUS_UNVERIFIED`, `WAIT_FOR_CONFIRMATION`, `VALID_CURRENT`; plus `alert_state` ∈ {`ALERT_NOT_ARMED`, `ARMED_NO_DELIVERY_RECEIPT`, `ARMED_DELIVERY_RECEIPTED`}. Every reason carries a machine code.
2. **Mechanics only under VALID_CURRENT.** Any other state suppresses sizing, R:R, reward/risk and order language on every surface (`suppress_mechanics`), keeps the levels visibly `historical` or `conditional`, records the invalidation reason, and asks for a new validated setup. A plan's stop below its old entry never establishes validity at the current price. NO ACTION must be compatible with every rationale and UI field.
3. **Validity, eligibility, house policy, tax assessment and execution authorization are distinct states.** `HELD_POLICY_BLOCK` (add-to-position policy vs flat re-entry policy, dust named separately), `HOUSE_WASH_HOLD` (a house 30-day hold after ANY taxable sell — labelled as policy; when the sell was a verified GAIN the record says "no wash-sale rule applies"), `TAX_STATUS_UNVERIFIED` (a taxable sell exists, lots unknown — conservative review state, no legal conclusion), and wash-sale RISK only for a verified LOSS with acquisitions in any account (IRA included) inside ±30 days. Missing evidence yields UNVERIFIED, never a prohibition.
4. **Quotes.** Age is shown exactly (minutes under an hour), judged by market session; an operator-quoted price that differs by more than `TRADEAI_QUOTE_CONFLICT_PCT` is disclosed as a second print and reconciled by time, never silently replaced. A Friday/last-session print is held over a closed market up to 72 h and labelled `LAST_SESSION_HELD`.
5. **Order semantics.** A buy limit at or above the market is marketable and fills immediately; when price is below the zone, "buy-limit in zone" is stated as `MARKETABLE_IMMEDIATE_FILL`. A desired reclaim is a conditional advisory/alert (price cross above zone low), never converted to an order. Venue order support is not represented until verified.
6. **Alerts.** A conversational offer is not monitoring. The card states what is armed from the `watch_alerts` store (`ALERT_NOT_ARMED` unless a row exists; delivery receipt named when present). An alert may watch for new evidence but cannot reactivate an invalidated plan: a fresh plan version and gate re-evaluation are required. The evaluator (`watch_alerts_eval`) skips a stale print for its session instead of firing on it.
7. **Wakes.** Prompt event-driven cognition stays on the existing bus → wake-store → dispatcher lane (PR #1231): correlation id = bus event id; priority-FIFO dispatch; dead-letter after 3 lease recoveries; hourly persistent wake remains recovery/reconciliation. Installed unit/cron/queued message ≠ successful wake; a wake is proved by receipt. No wake route grants broker, order, risk, stop, credential or 2FA authority.
8. **Cases.** A contradiction between surfaces becomes a `watch_contradiction` case in the existing case store (`record_decision_integrity_case.py`, dry-run default), linking inbound lineage, served SHA, plan and quote versions, validator states and the corrected answer. A case is not evidence of a trade outcome or a learned behaviour change.

## Consequences
- Surfaces agree by construction (same validator object): Telegram card, desk row `integrity`, advisory sizing, alert state.
- Existing thresholds remain in their modules; the validator reads, it does not redefine. The five disagreeing TTLs are documented as a follow-up to consolidate through `EvidenceFreshnessPolicy@v1`.
- Additive fields only (`row["integrity"]`, `row["positions"]`, `advisory["historical_plan"]`, `advisory["decision_integrity"]`). Rollback = revert the branch; no schema, flag, cron or data change.
- Operator-only: arming alerts, recording the SCHD case with `--apply`, moving the reconcile launcher to CURRENT, enforcing the release-grant binding on the served deploy path.

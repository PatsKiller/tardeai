# GAP — what stands between this system and the next level, 2026-09-12

Campaign `trade-ai-maturity-overnight-20260912`. Gaps are ordered by what they
block, not by effort. Every number was measured on this host during the window.

---

## G1 · The read plane does not serve what the batch plane produces — **blocks L1**

`persistent_overlay.OVERLAY_RELS` declares which trees must be one store, and
`cio_phase2_exact_main_deploy.sh` applies that rule to the **release**. It was
never applied to the **canonical tree**, where 106 active cron lines execute.

```
served   data/runtime/sector_momentum_latest.json  inode 9633692  2026-08-25
producer data/runtime/sector_momentum_latest.json  inode 3573658  2026-09-11
```

164 served files are behind their producers across five trees, and **19 files in
`data/runtime` alone flow the other way**, the served copy newer by up to 31.9
days. That bidirectionality is the whole difficulty: no single-direction merge is
safe, and `data/portfolios/state` — 48 stale files, one 151 days — holds live
holdings and sits behind a guard scope this campaign was not granted.

**Why it matters beyond L1.** It is the reason the Oscillator board serves a
17-day-old reading; it is why MVL counters read low (`sentinel_reviews.jsonl` is
140 rows on the producer plane and 104 on the served one); and it is a recurrence
of a defect a health agent previously "fixed" 69 times while reporting `ok: True`.

**Closing it:** adjudicate direction per file, then apply the overlay to the
canonical tree exactly as deploy already does for the release — preserve, never
merge, never delete. `scripts/check_state_root_split.py` ships in PR #975 and
defaults to `OVERLAY_RELS` itself, so a newly declared tree cannot fork silently.
Full procedure and the per-file direction census:
`STATE_ROOT_RECONCILIATION_RUNBOOK.md`.

## G2 · Nothing that is written is falsifiable — **blocks L4**

Of 224 durable commitment records, 99 are predictions and **0 of the 99 can be
scored**. Every one carries the falsifier

> "observation contradicts claim within horizon"

over a claim that asserts only that a review occurred. That names no observable,
so no later evidence could contradict it. The earliest `due_at` is 2026-09-17;
when that date arrives, nothing will be scoreable.

The machinery is no longer the gap. PR #975 adds the scheduled pass that was
missing — `evaluate_outcome` previously had one caller which ran it on the same
line that minted the commitment, and **0 cron entries and 0 systemd units**
revisited a due commitment. The gap is now upstream: the producer must write a
falsifier that names an observable and a claim that could fail.

**Closing it:** make the commitment producer refuse a vacuous falsifier at write
time, the way `validate_author_judgment` already refuses a missing one. A
prediction that cannot fail should not reach the store.

## G3 · The judgment lane is starved by design — **blocks L3**

One undifferentiated daily pool, claimed first-come first-served:

```
advisory_desk_opinion     1019 calls   $0.83435
hermes_external_research   164 calls   $0.30519
others                                 $0.00697
                                       --------
                                       $1.14651   of $1.50
```

From 20:00Z every hourly L3 judgment refused `budget_cap` and the research
producer refused `BUDGET_REFUSED:CALLER_DAILY_CAP`. Per-process caps do not
constrain this — they sum to **$15.48 against a $1.50 pool**, a 10× oversubscription.

Three further facts make it structural rather than unlucky:

- `l3_judgment_author` has **no row at all** in `llm_process_config`, and an
  unregistered lane had `daily_cost_cap_usd=None`, which **skipped the process
  check entirely**. The lane with no declared budget was the least constrained
  caller on the host.
- `LLM_GLOBAL_DAILY_USD_CAP` is read from *each caller's own environment* —
  five cron lines pass `0.50`, two pass `7.00`. A global cap each caller declares
  for itself is an honour system.
- The L3 cache could never hit, so every hourly wake paid for a fresh call on
  unchanged evidence (fixed in PR #975).

**Closing it:** PR #975 ships per-lane floors, inert until configured. Setting a
floor for `l3_judgment_author` and `governed_research_producer`, registering the
author lane in `llm_process_config`, and sourcing the global cap from one place
rather than from each caller would close it.

## G4 · Sentinel reviews cite nothing — **blocks MVL**

| clause | bar | measured |
|---|---|---|
| retrieval on eligible Sentinel reviews | ≥95% | **0 of 140 = 0.0%** |
| Darwin scoring on eligible artifacts | ≥95% | 136 of 136 = 100% |
| measured Sentinel false-positive rate | measured | fail rate 4/140; **FP rate not measurable** |

Not one Sentinel review carries a retrieval, evidence-ref or memory-fact field.
A review that cites nothing cannot be audited, and a false-positive rate needs
the four FAIL verdicts adjudicated — no adjudication path exists, so 2.9% is a
fail rate and must not be reported as an FP rate.

Separately, the "100 reviewed Watch artifacts" clause is not met by these 136:
they are agent advisory artifacts (mandates like `capital_deployment_idle_cash`),
not Watch artifacts. The corpus is wrong, not merely small.

## G6 · The batch plane is not on the served SHA — **blocks a single epoch**

The dev tree is 2 commits behind `origin/main`, and 106 active cron lines execute
from it. Only one of those lines invokes a script that differs between the two
SHAs, so the runtime impact today is small — but no single epoch identity covers
both planes, which is a precondition for every acceptance claim above.

Fast-forwarding it needs guard scope `maintree`, which this campaign was not
granted, and two tracked files must never take main's version
(`infra/searxng/core-config/settings.yml` is the live bind-mounted config;
`config/hermes_score_weights.yaml` is newer locally than upstream).

The maturity spine is unaffected: the hourly wake, the research producer, the
selection feed and the circulation service all resolve `CURRENT`, so a promote
reaches every lane this campaign touched.

## G7 · Acceptance was weakest where authority was highest — **closed**

`ai_local_acceptance.sh` probed the pre-push hook with
`TRADEAI_REMOTE_PUSH_AUTHORIZED=0`, but the hook treats a live operator
`git-push` grant as authorization. With a grant active the probe failed and
`set -e` aborted **every later gate** — release-equivalent, lane registry, CIO
hardening. Every campaign holding a push grant therefore ran almost no local
acceptance.

Fixed in PR #975, and it paid for itself twice within the hour: the first run
surfaced 14 undeclared schedules including the entire maturity spine, and the
second caught a broken SOP control-surface attestation that GitHub CI would
otherwise have failed on.

## Four things fixed tonight, and what each proves

1. **`source_sha` on durable rows.** Every organic wake now stamps the exact
   served SHA. Before tonight all 48 read `unknown`, which made exact-SHA
   acceptance impossible regardless of behaviour.
2. **The research producer had never produced on schedule.** Its hourly cron
   passed no `--execute`, and `--dry-run` is argparse's default, so every run
   resolved targets, printed intent and exited 0. One flag. It now produces 15
   objects an hour and the wakes consume them.
3. **Memory could not be found.** 488 of 930 durable rows carry no
   `subject_guid`; matching required one. Resolution by symbol makes 145 rows
   reachable.
4. **Making memory findable broke the wake.** `refuse_stale_memory` had never
   executed — staleness derives from the newest loaded fact, and every wake
   loaded zero. Armed, it aborted a real wake. Stale memory now degrades to
   empty instead of ending the decision.

Each of the four is the same shape: **a component reporting success while doing
nothing** (§3).

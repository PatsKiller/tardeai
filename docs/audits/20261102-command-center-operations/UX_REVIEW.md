## Readiness judgment and limits

[CODE] The application contains substantial useful evidence: a single Do-now queue, URL-backed views and detail sheets, source clocks, job completion evidence, incident ownership/history, release pins and per-PR actions. The primary failure is information priority and inconsistent semantics across these surfaces. An operator must translate multiple versions of Health, Jobs, Agents, Work and Deploys before reaching the reason a critical pipeline failed. The dashboard should not yet claim an operator can establish platform health within 60 seconds.

## Home during a market event

[CODE] The market-risk banner is first on default Home (`pages/HomeDoNow.tsx:194–200`), followed by the summary/header and Do-now queue. The queue already correctly warns “not an all-clear” for missing sources (`components/home/DoNowQueue.tsx:65–76`) and has Protect money/Decide/Fix system filters. Keep those safeguards.

[CODE] Default queue shows top seven and sorts by money at risk then urgency (`DoNowQueue.tsx:9–12,28–33,126–128`). A critical platform outage with no dollar amount should also receive a persistent critical summary independent of the money-sorted decision queue. System pulse is later, after account/mover/opportunity content (`HomeDoNow.tsx:225–369`), while Agents and Hermes is a collapsed drawer describing runs over 30 days (`:394–395`), not current engineering execution. Neither should be the sole way to discover an unhandled P0.

[CODE] No Acknowledge on YELLOW is consistent with implementation: `lib/marketRisk.ts::bannerVisible` requires evaluated, fresh ORANGE/RED, and only that banner has the episode acknowledgment (`MarketRiskBanner.tsx:139–147`). Shadow/carried tiers explicitly have nothing to acknowledge. An orange/red design example does not require an acknowledge action on a yellow summary. Needed clarity is the tier’s last evaluation, relevant source session and whether an actual alert episode exists. Never change risk thresholds or fabricate an episode to make the mockup match.

Proposed above-fold content (design target, not implemented): one compact line for current market tier and source age; one platform incident strip with severity, impact, owner, age, alert-delivery confirmation and current remediation; one positions/P&L freshness label per fact; the existing decision queue with the highest-priority actions and explicit missing-source warning. On Jobs/Health/Incidents, operational triage comes before market metrics, provider balance, expanded provenance or generic approval copy. Critical summaries should remain visible at 390×844 and 1440×900 without hiding access to data gaps.

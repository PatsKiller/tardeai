## Diagnostic contract and remaining limits

[CODE] scripts/lib/job_failure_diagnostics.py::diagnostics_for_run is a pure no-I/O projection named JobFailureDiagnostics@v1. It exposes safe failure reason/type; traceback availability, source and missing reason; exit and duration provenance; UTC start/finish/last-success; symbolic host role with private identity suppressed; output-verdict subreason; reaper provenance; evidence gaps; and a remediation key/suggestion. It refuses conflicting embedded run identities. Redaction precedes truncation.

Private host/worker identifiers, credentials, account details and raw stdout/stderr are excluded from this report and its adjacent JSON. Host role is deliberately symbolic. A receipt can truthfully state that no traceback or child exit was observed; this needs a remediation owner rather than invented evidence.

[CODE] The timeout patch prevents one future diagnostic-loss path. It cannot recover output after the executor process itself disappeared. No evidence here establishes why those executor processes were lost, nor whether their children produced effects. Governance and exactly-once effect reconciliation remain in place.

Required release acceptance: independent review of exact commits; governed draft PR publication; CI and approved release; observe the served Jobs detail and a naturally scheduled receipt; bind every remediation key to a real ticket/PR; investigate remaining protected causes only with the appropriate scoped grant. No local test establishes production recovery.

the gate's vocabulary, with `execution_language` outranking everything —
fail-closed is a state, not one signal among several. The report now feeds it.

## Step 3 — why it was not forced

The two clean eligible jobs (SPCX, ARKX) both decide **`grok_critique`**, not
`flash`: they already have completed VALID artifacts awaiting critique. The
brief allows Grok critique as the live hop when that is the decision.

The Grok lane is available — OAuth proxy on port 8645, `ready: true`,
`billing: free_oauth`. So the blocker is not credentials.

The blocker is that **no live Grok critique path exists**.
`research_quality.critique()` is a deterministic lint with no network call, and
nothing wires a critique through the Grok lane. Making that call would mean
*building* a vendor call site and choosing its prompt, retry and cost
accounting — designing the integration, not taking "one live hop" through an
existing one. That is well outside what was authorised, so it was not done.

## Behavior

`lib.sector_signals` defines relative strength as the sector return minus SPY's return, in percentage points, over exactly 5, 20 and 60 SPY sessions. Missing closes leave a gap; a ten-session history never becomes a twenty-session return. Both longer horizons determine the label. One-day quotes and five-session strength are context only.

| 20 sessions | 60 sessions | State / trend | CIO posture |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nonnegative | Nonnegative | Leading / Strengthening | Favoured |
| Negative | Negative | Lagging / Weakening | Review |
| Nonnegative | Negative | Improving / Recovering | Research watch |
| Negative | Nonnegative | Weakening / Cooling | Neutral |
| Both zero | Both zero | Flat | Neutral |
| Either absent | Any | Not measured | Not measured, with reason |

These are advisory sector labels, without target weights or allocation changes. The sector writer, broker projection, Defense snapshot reader, and CIO opportunity reader share this definition. The writer retains the real source date. Its former `max(source_date, today)` date substitution is removed.

The board-18 table keeps industry/research detail available in its expandable section. Source gaps, stale observations, exposure, and screened leaders remain visible. Missing screening counts stay unknown. Pullback metrics expose explanations on tap; no browser-side score calculation remains. Leaders are limited to the displayed screened universe, ranked by the same 20-session return difference. They are not a market-wide leader list or CIO recommendations. Exposure dollars and percentages are copied from the holdings owner's `resolved_sectors` fields; the page does not recompute a portfolio denominator.

Pullback creation intersects configured tiers with `trigger`, so old database configuration cannot re-enable WATCH proposals. Queue reconciliation withdraws WATCH rows even when ordinary plan reconciliation is disabled. It retains the proposal row, writes `rejection_reason`/timestamps, and logs each successful withdrawal. A concurrent fill or status transition prevents the guarded update. A failed withdrawal write fails the lane, rather than reporting a successful no-op. Rows attached to a paper trade are excluded. The UI requires both `tier=trigger` and `proposal_live=true` to say a proposal is queued. A saved ID alone carries no queue claim.

No worker live run, queue cleanup, broker call, send, schedule edit, policy edit, push, merge or release was performed. Existing queued rows require the next authorized scan/monitor after integration; this change does not claim they were already withdrawn in production.

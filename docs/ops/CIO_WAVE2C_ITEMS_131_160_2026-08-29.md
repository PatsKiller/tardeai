## The gap: the books were separate but anonymous

The standing rail is "two re-entry books, labeled and separate." The *separate*
half has held since #584. The *labeled* half was only half true.

`cio_reentry_surface_labels` already defines both surfaces canonically, and each
producer stamps its own book:

| | book | producer | labelled at source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | former holdings vs exit trigger | `cio_investment_product.build_reentry_book` | yes |
| **B** | candidates vs cash-stage R:R | `cio_desk_depth.build_reentry_book` | yes |

But **neither book's labels reached `/api/v3/cio/home`**, and the CC named only
Surface A. So a reader saw two re-entry numbers — `reentry_total 25` and
`queue_reentry_total 43` — with nothing saying which question each answers.
That is how two books get merged in someone's head while the code keeps them
apart.

`home.reentry_books` now carries both, read **from the canonical module** rather
than re-worded locally: a second copy of the label text is a second definition
waiting to drift. `merged: false` is on the payload, and each book disclaims the
other's question. The CC renders Surface B beneath the Surface A card.

### A wrong turn worth recording

My first attempt labelled `build_opportunity_book` as "Surface B" with text I
wrote myself. That was wrong twice over: Surface B is the *desk cash-stage*
book from `cio_desk_depth`, not the opportunity book, and the canonical labels
already existed. It would have created a second, conflicting definition of B.
Reverted before it went anywhere.

# StopTrailingControlPanel Design

## Layout
Per open trade row:
Symbol | Account | Strategy | Family | Entry | Current | DB Stop | Broker Proof | R | Current Tier | Next Tier | Time-Stop | Status | Action

## Filters
All | Stop proof missing | Time-stop overdue | Trailing eligible | Stop changed recently | No adapter | Healthy

## Drill-down
Click row → show trailing policy, tier thresholds, recent stop changes, time-stop details
